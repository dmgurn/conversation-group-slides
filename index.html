Add your lesson slides

This happens once. The slides are saved on this iPad, so lessons open without internet.

  1. Tap Choose slides.
  2. In iCloud Drive, pick EnglishConnect Slides for iPad.zip. You can also select lesson PDFs named like EC1 EN L08 - Everyday Common Items.pdf.
  3. Wait for all 100 lessons to be added. It takes about a minute.

Your pen marks are saved on this iPad. Make a backup now and then so you can restore them if you get a new iPad.
